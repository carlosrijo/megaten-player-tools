## What's new in 0.16.0

- **Community Builds** (new): share your build and browse other players' builds. **Publish build** on the Overview sends your character's build (with the digitalize demon you pick) or one demon's build to the gallery. You can update it or take it down at any time.
- **Community Builds** pages: **Demon Builds** and **Character Builds** are in the left bar, below the other tools. Each is a grid of cards with chips, search, sorting and pages. A character build opens like the Character Planner (read only): stats beside the picture, the expertise bar with every rank and its skills, the demons, the switch skills, the gear grid and the key stats, with **Open in planner**. A demon build opens like the Overview's Demon user view: its card, its Mitamas and its key stats and affinities. You can copy the link or the build code, and report a build.
- **Overview**: a summoned partner's own skills and epitaphs (Pursuit chance, features, passives, Demon Force) now count in its totals, each named by what gives it.
- **Gear**: Soul Stone fusion sets show the name the stones' own text gives them (Lunar Calendar, Great Winter Triangle...).
- The passive groups on **Expertise** and **Digitalize** start collapsed.
- **BABEL on the web**: the Quest Tracker page shows the download banner, and the site has an icon.
