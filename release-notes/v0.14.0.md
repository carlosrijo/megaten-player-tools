## What's new in 0.14.0

- **BABEL on the web** (https://babel-7vg.pages.dev/): the databases and planners now also open in your browser, no download needed: Skills, Demons, Items, Quests, Key Items, Dungeons, DCM Locator, Demon Ranking, Dungeon Guides and the Fusion, Digitalize and Demon Planners. Your own character's pages, the Quest Tracker and the Character Planner still need the desktop app.
- **Fusion Planner**: "Find a recipe" is now part of the Fusion tree, and the demon picker has a clear button.
- The Digitalize Planner's goal search and the assistant's demon field also have a clear button.
