## What's new in 0.8.5

- **Quest Guide**: answers that lead to different outcomes are shown as a tree, with what each branch gives and what it does to your alignment (Uriel and Surt in Act 13, DB Tanaka in Act 5, and more). When the change depends on your alignment, it says so in Law, Neutral and Chaos terms.
- **Quest Guide**: answers that lead to another outcome are no longer marked as wrong, and wrong answers that change your alignment say how.
