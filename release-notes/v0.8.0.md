## What's new in 0.8.0

- New **Send feedback** button (speech bubble in the top bar): send a bug report, idea or question straight from the app, no GitHub account needed. Diagnostics files are attached, encrypted, unless you untick them.
- **Build Planner** (was Simulator): share a build as a code and import someone else's; build cards show a picture of every piece, and hovering one shows its tooltip with a "Changes to" comparison against what you wear.
- **Expertise**: a new "Expertise cap" section shows every cap raise from valuables and quests, which ones you have, and which are still open or ready to claim from Shiho.
- **Demon Database** and **Demon Rank** list the demon variants you can own (Open-minded Ishtar and 591 more), each with how to get it.
- **Overview**: delete old snapshots and cached demons.
- Status effects are named ("Immune to Instant Death", "Charm success rate") instead of numbered.
- Gear tooltips show a picture of where each part comes from: the crystals and the set's pieces.
- Every soul and tarot is named by its crystal, and sets no longer show internal numbers.
- The sidebar scrolls on its own when the window is short.
- Fixed: the Overview now counts the quest bonus's Critical and Limit Break chance and power.
