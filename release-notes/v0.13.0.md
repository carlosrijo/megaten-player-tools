## What's new in 0.13.0

- **Fusion Planner**: a new **Fusion tree** view shows the whole way to a demon as a pyramid, down to demons you can contract all year or get from a Summon PG that an NPC trades outside events. The easiest demons come first, levels are free (Magical Golden Apples) so each demon shows the lowest level that works, and you can click a demon for every other way to get it, mark "I already have it", zoom and drag, and see the fusions in order and what to get. Find a recipe also has an "Easiest to get first" order and Contract / PG marks.
- **Overview**: a **Player / Demon user** switch above the picture, kept per character. For a character whose gear boosts the partner, the Demon view makes the partner the main card: its level, race, alignment and Mitama, stats, skills, Demon Force, features and demon equipment, and the rebirth radar. Gear marks who each piece boosts (partner, you or both), and the key stats and affinities show the partner's numbers.
- **Character Planner**: digitalize with a demon from the Demon Planner. The demon picker is now a dialog with two parts: **My demons** and **From Demon Planner** (each saved build as the demon it ends as). It has a search that sorts by a stat or effect, chips, and each demon's stats, effects, skills, Demon Force, equipment and rebirth ranks, plus what picking it would change. The Demon Planner's Import demon opens the same dialog, and imported demons keep their equipment.
- **Overview**: the affinity radar draws the boost over the resistances, and a key stat with no sources is greyed out and can't be opened. The rank radar reads Pre-Mitama / Post-Mitama.
- The whole app uses the same thin scrollbar as the left bar.
