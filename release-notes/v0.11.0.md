## What's new in 0.11.0

- **Item Database** (was the Gear Database): every item in the game, not only gear: consumables, materials, vouchers and boxes, tarot and soul crystals, demon equipment, appearance items and key items. Each one shows the game's description, what a box or ticket can give, a crystal's tarot and soul lines, and the quests that hand it over, with a link to the quest in the Quest Database.
- **Item Database**: chips filter by kind and by the game's own item class. A **Filters** panel adds who can use it, alignment, where it comes from, weapon range, affinity and a level limit. Click a column header to sort.
- **Item Database** and **Skill Database**: long lists come in pages of 100, 200 or 500 rows.
