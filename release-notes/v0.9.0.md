## What's new in 0.9.0

- **Quest Tracker** (new tool): follows the quests you have in progress live from the game: the step you are on, kill counts, which talks of a step are done, and what there is to do in the zone you are in. Hide and show each quest.
- **Quest panel**: a small window over the game with every quest in progress, compact and collapsible, so you can see the next step without leaving the game.
- **Quest Database**: the Quest Guide is now the Quest Database, next to the other databases. A step says what has to happen first (who takes you to its place, what makes a hidden person appear), and people show their picture before their name.
- **Overview**: Export build gives the build you are wearing as a share code.
- **Bonuses, Gear and Expertise**: more effects are counted, and their conditions are spelled out: which skills they work with, the expertise rank they need, HP, alignment or partner.
