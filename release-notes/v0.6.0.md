## What's new in 0.6.0

- **Digitalize**: boost pictures, locked levels, a tinted Features picture, a two-column demon card with square demon bars, and feature and equipment icons.
- **Bonuses**: VIT/INT and SPD now count against cooldown and incantation time; the summoned partner's gift to its summoner is counted; the Partner's cap comes from Limit Mastery Lv3+; only the chosen skill of an expertise rank that offers a choice is counted.
- Support damage dealt and taken are shown beside Close-range, Long-range and Spell. Toggle skills are labelled on the Bonuses page and Overview.
- **Affinity radar**: Resistance alone fits small resistances, and the legend filters.
- Character names with spaces and symbols are detected.
