## What's new in 0.1.0

The first public release: one portable program, nothing to install.

- **Overview** of your character: key stats, affinities, gear, expertise, with a "What changed" comparison between snapshots.
- **Expertise** page with a summary of points, counts and class rank per expertise.
- **Build Planner** (called Simulator then): try other gear, expertise ranks and demons, and save builds.
- **Demon Database**: every demon with stats, affinities, features, Mitama form, skills and where to contract it. Filter by alignment.
- **Fusion Planner**: fuse two or three demons, or find every recipe for a demon, with plugin names and where to get them.
- **Dungeon Explorer**: every dungeon with its enemies by difficulty, their drops and end chests.
- **Skill Database**: every skill grouped by the game's action types.
- Black Frost, drawn from the game's own model, helps out in the corner.
