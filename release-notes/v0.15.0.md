## What's new in 0.15.0

- **Overview** (Demon user view): a Mitama-fused partner shows its Mitamas per kind as coloured chips under its card, and a new **Mitama** section shows its kinds against its Mitama rank, what it was fused with, a row per rune, the rank bonus, the sets that are on and the bonuses of the placed Mitamas. The key stats and affinities count the partner's Mitamas as its own. The partner's rank radar no longer carries the Pre-Mitama / Post-Mitama labels.
- **Overview**: the digitalize demon's name opens the Character Planner's demon dialog (your COMP demons, with **Load demons from the game** at its foot, and a remove button on demons no longer in the COMP) in place of the dropdown.
- **BABEL on the web**: the character pages now show a demo character under a **Download BABEL** banner, and the game's pictures load on the site.
