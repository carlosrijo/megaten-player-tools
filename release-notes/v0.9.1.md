## What's new in 0.9.1

- **Expertise**: a new **Planner** view (the switch at the top: Your expertise / Planner). Every expertise gets a slider starting from your ranks, and the page lists the skills you gain or lose (chains too) and every number that moves. Your plan is kept per character.
- **Expertise** and **Build Planner**: a changed expertise gets a coloured dot after its name (new, changed, or down to 0) and shows "was X-X" under the rank. The unlock lists show skill pictures and tooltips, passives in italics, and "or" between a rank's choices.
- **Expertise**: the skill icons in the Summary and in closed groups show the game-style tooltip: name, active or passive, expertise and rank, and the game's description.
