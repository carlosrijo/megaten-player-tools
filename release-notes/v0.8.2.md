## What's new in 0.8.2

- **Digitalize Planner**: races you haven't unlocked can be goals. Search a race in the goal box and tick "Unlock <race>": it's planned with your other goals, and the list and goal card say what opens the race, ticking the needs you already meet.
- **Digitalize Planner**: each race's tier shows as a coloured rarity ladder on the race chips, in the goal dropdown and on the goal card, in both the dark and light themes.
