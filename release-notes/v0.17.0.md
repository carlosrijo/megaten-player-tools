## What's new in 0.17.0

- **DCM Tracker** (new, next to the DCM Locator): read from the game, it marks each DCM as **Registered**, **Not registered** or **In your bag**, with chips for each and a switch that hides the unregistered DCMs you already have in your bag.
- **Fusion Planner**: every recipe lists each special fusion with the demons it names (for example Odin: Valkyrie + Yatagarasu + Sleipnir), besides the easier versions. Click a whole recipe card to put it in the Fusion tree; the button now says **Use this**.
- **Overview** and **Bonuses**: Iron Man now boosts the partner's Mitama bonuses, as it does in the game. The Your partner rows on **Bonuses** include the partner's Mitama bonuses (rank bonus, sets and placed Mitamas) in the Passives column, one line per source, with an orb of the four Mitamas.
- **Overview**: stat numbers sit over their bars, so a long demon name no longer pushes the partner's values over the next label. The rebirth radar sits closer to the stats, and the Summoned chip is smaller and beside "Your partner".
- Fixed: the Player / Demon user switch on the Overview animates again, Iron Man is no longer labelled an active skill, and a long source list on **Bonuses** wraps instead of running over the value.
