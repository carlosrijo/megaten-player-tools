## What's new in 0.2.0

- The app is now a native program (it was rewritten in Rust): snapshots and rebuilds are faster, and the exe is smaller.
- Troubleshooting tools: version info, a local log, crash reports, a self-check and a support bundle.
- The Digitalize dropdown lists both demons of one kind and level, and marks the one you actually have saved.
- Gear Database shows item icons, with detail sections named and ordered like the Gear page tooltips.
- Build Planner can search pieces by stat.
- BABEL always opens on the Overview.
- Fixes: your own max and current HP and MP were read the wrong way round on snapshots, the logged-in character is detected more reliably, the last item records are kept when they cannot be read, and the page now says why saved stats are shown. Plus a round of smaller fixes from an overnight bug hunt.
