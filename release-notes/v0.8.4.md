## What's new in 0.8.4

- **Demon Database**: a demon's details open on **Where to get it**: whether fusion makes it (how many recipes, the cheapest, and a button to the Fusion Planner), each special fusion with its plugin, where to get each plugin, and a button that opens a quest in the Quest Guide.
- **Demon Database**: a **Show variants** toggle hides the Lucky, Inexperienced, Open-minded and other variants and says how many are hidden. Your choice is remembered.
- **Quest Guide**: steps list every talk they need (for example Kiara, Nora, Lorie and Izo in Act 1: Demonization), questions show which other answers also work and which don't move the quest on, and the wording matches the game's 19 November dialogue update.
